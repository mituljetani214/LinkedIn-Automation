## Post Draft

Design systems matter more in the AI era, not less.

Many people think AI will make design systems less important because screens can be generated faster.

I think the opposite.

When teams create faster, consistency becomes harder.

When AI helps generate more options, quality control becomes more important.

When product surfaces grow, small inconsistencies become product debt.

A design system is not just a component library.

It is a shared decision system.

It tells the team:

- how patterns should behave
- when to reuse components
- when to create something new
- how states should work
- how accessibility should be handled
- how design translates to development
- how the product should feel across screens

In complex SaaS products, this matters a lot.

On Munim, a unified design system helped the product feel less like separate modules and more like one coherent tool.

On Group8A, a design system helped keep a 30+ page dashboard consistent while improving handoff between design and development.

AI can help us move faster.

But design systems help us move faster without losing direction.

That is the real advantage.
