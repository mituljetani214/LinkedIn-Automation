## Post Draft

Figma is no longer just where designers make screens.

It is becoming a product operating space.

After the latest Config announcements, one thing feels clear:

The canvas is moving closer to the full product workflow.

Not only frames.

Also:

- motion
- code layers
- AI agents
- generative plugins
- reusable workflows
- design systems
- feedback
- prototyping
- team context

For product designers, this is a big shift.

But I do not think the lesson is "learn every new tool immediately."

The real lesson is:

Designers need stronger product thinking.

Because when tools become faster, weak decisions also become faster.

If your information architecture is unclear, AI can generate more unclear screens.

If your design system has no rules, AI can create more inconsistency.

If your workflow is not understood, automation can amplify the wrong thing.

So the future designer is not only someone who can create visuals.

The future designer needs to understand:

- user behavior
- business goals
- technical constraints
- system rules
- workflow design
- AI-assisted decision making

Tools are becoming more powerful.

That means our thinking has to become sharper.
