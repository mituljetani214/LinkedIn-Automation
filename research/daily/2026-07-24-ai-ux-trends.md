## Top Trends

1. AI is moving from design assistance to workflow integration.
   Figma's 2026 announcements emphasize agents, generative plugins, Weave tools, code layers, motion, and reusable workflows directly inside the canvas. The larger shift is that design tools are becoming operating spaces where design, code, motion, and AI workflows connect.

2. Enterprise UX is becoming operator-first.
   Enterprise products are no longer just launched and handed off. The strongest enterprise UX work now focuses on trained operators, workflow primitives, dashboards, audit trails, identity, alerts, and year-five operation, not just year-one launch.

3. AI in UX is shifting from experimentation to integration.
   Designers are moving beyond "try this AI tool" into quality control, curation, leadership, and repeatable workflows. AI is useful when it improves research, discovery, documentation, ideation, and speed, but human judgment still decides what should ship.

4. Design systems are becoming governance systems.
   In the AI era, design systems are not only component libraries. They help teams scale taste, maintain consistency, guide AI-generated output, reduce drift, and preserve product quality while moving faster.
